## RAKT 0.1.6

**Generate a band. Play it live.**

- **Studio opens on Generator.** Pick a genre and press EDIT to generate a new arrangement without replacing your chosen sounds. Use Arrangement for Tempo, Density and Evolve. Switch to Groove for Swing and the Drums, Bass and Chords styles.
- **GLO Live has seven effects.** Hold the upper keys for Riser, Beat Repeat, Hype, Ambience, Radio, Phaser and Low Pass. The band keeps playing while you mix, solo parts, cue fills or drop the drums.
- **Reactive faceplates.** The Generator waves respond to Genre, Density and Swing. GLO's charcoal waves and rainbow accents react when you hold a performance effect. Settings can turn off the timed motion.
- **Clearer rack controls.** Zoomed panels keep knob labels and values in view. FX Space is in Sound → Mix, and ALGORITHM scrolls through ARP as part of the Studio rack.
