## RAKT 0.1.4

- **Studio Song** shows the current chord, progression, key, meter and tempo. Its four knobs control Genre, Key, Tempo and Meter. **Record** opens on Saved Takes for quick playback.
- **Studio performances** can use genre-specific arpeggios and octave choices. **GLO** gives each part a dedicated level knob.
- **GLO Live effects** add C5 Riser, D5 Beat Repeat, E5 Echo Out and F5 High-pass Sweep. Hold a key to apply its effect. While holding D5, turn **PRESETS** to change the repeat rate. The song continues underneath and the USB master mix captures the effects.
