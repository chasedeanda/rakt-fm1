## RAKT 0.1.7

- **ARP controls on the faceplate.** Turn SELECT to choose Chords or Layer, and press ARP to switch between Pattern and Timing.
- **Song brings harmony and progression together.** Open Song and turn SELECT to reach Harmony or Progression. Press EDIT on Progression to open your draft.
- **FX Space has its own panel.** In Sound, turn ALGORITHM one step past Mix and use K1 to scale chorus, delay and reverb across the band.
- **Distinct rack finishes.** Static contours and waves vary by faceplate. ARP twinkles pink and FX Space twinkles blue. Turn Motion Off to keep both still. The Sound Library sticker and RAKT mascot artwork are gone.
