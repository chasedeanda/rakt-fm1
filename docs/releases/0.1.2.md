## RAKT 0.1.2

- **Studio recording** captures Chords, Bass, Layer and Drums in one take, including arpeggios, live keys and fills. Replay the latest three takes from the Record rack.
- **Independent Studio FX** give each of the four musicians its own preset and effects settings.
- **Rack navigation** uses the slide-in for page changes and keeps rapid scrolling within the rack steady.
- **Boot sound** adds a sharp room snare to the rising electric-key strum and boosts the complete cue by 50%. [Listen to the boot cue](https://github.com/chasedeanda/rakt-fm1/blob/v0.1.2/docs/audio/rakt-boot-chime.wav).
