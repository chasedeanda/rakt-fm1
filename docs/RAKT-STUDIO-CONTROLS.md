# RAKT Studio controls

Turn **SELECT** on an overview to switch between Studio, Sound, Record and Help. Turn **ALGORITHM** to move between units. Each tab keeps two neighboring full-width units in view and scrolls when the selection moves past an edge. Switching tabs recalls the rack position. **SEQ** jumps to the outboard gear in Studio and steps through its units.

The arrow and the light strip on the right show the selected unit and its position in the rack. The control strip below the bay shows K1–K4. Press **SEL** to zoom in. **ALGORITHM** moves between units at either zoom level. In detail, **SELECT** turns pages, picks the musician on ARP, or switches tabs on single-page units. **HOME** backs out to the same unit. Press **HOME** again from the overview to return to Studio. **SEL** applies the shown choice on panels that say **SEL Apply**.

## Studio

**PLAY** starts or stops the band. Studio opens on **Generator**. Turn **ALGORITHM** one step to **Song**, which shows the current chord name and Roman numeral above the key, scale, meter and tempo. The progression name is beside the panel title. A held manual chord takes over the readout until you release it. Use **K1** for Genre, **K2** for Key, **K3** for Tempo and **K4** for Meter. Press **SEL** for a larger chord display and knob readouts.

### Rack design

The rack shares the startup screen's graphite rails, slotted screws, recessed displays, and four-pixel gaps. Faceplates use distinct waves and contour patterns alongside woven grilles and glitter.

| Unit | Hardware | Readout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Song | Recessed display and four knobs | Live chord, progression, key, scale, meter and tempo |
| Chords | Bronze face, stitched grille, polished rotary caps | Style or patch and an amber signal lamp |
| Bass | Dark slate face and inset rubber knobs | Style or patch and a green activity display |
| Drums | Taller steel-grey face with a row of trigger lamps | Style or kit, plus Kick, Snare, Hats and Percussion hits |
| Layer | Slim plum face with horizontal faders | Style or patch and the part's level |
| Generator | Green face with a wave pattern | Arrangement, Groove and output level |
| ARP | Violet glitter face | Chords or Layer, Pattern or Timing, and arpeggiator step |
| Mix | Four-channel fader bank from the startup rack | Set levels and muted channels |
| FX Space | Blue glitter face with a single amount knob | Scale the band's chorus, delay and reverb |
| Record | Full-width, two-bar lanes and a Saved Takes unit | Notes, hits, playhead and take slots |

The right-side rack index shows which units are visible. Tabs slide into the cabinet. A detail page slides its controls beneath the fixed faceplate. The ARP and FX Space faces twinkle while selected. Motion Off settles the layout immediately and keeps the glitter still.

### Genre

On **Generator** or **Song**, turn **K1** to choose a genre. **PRESETS** also works from the Studio overview. Genre changes the harmonic and rhythmic vocabulary of the whole band, including the two playing styles available for each musician. A genre change queues a new take for the next bar during playback. Your current tempo continues while the band plays. Open **Sound → Sound Library** in Genre mode and press **SEL** to load the curated palette, or press **EDIT** on the Sound overview to cycle through its alternatives.

| Genre | Playing character |
| --- | --- |
| Dream Pop | Sustained or pulsing chords, melodic bass, a soft beat and sparse upper figures |
| Indie | Chopped or ringing chords, offbeat bass, room drums and a chime line |
| New Wave | Short chord stabs, octave bass, a machine beat and stepped synth figures |
| Pop Punk | Palm-muted attacks, open chords, bass riffs locked to the kick, half-time and straight grooves, and a clean picked Layer |
| Lo-Fi | Soft keys, a late bass pocket, half-time drums and short melodic fragments |
| Ambient Soundscape | Slow harmony, pedal bass, sparse percussion and evolving swells |

### Song pages and Generator

The Studio rack runs from **Generator**, **Song**, and **ARP** down to the four musicians. **SEQ** cycles through the first three panels. Press **SEL** on **Song**, then turn **SELECT** through **Overview**, **Harmony**, and **Progression**. On **Generator**, the **Arrangement** tab holds Genre, Tempo, Density, and Evolve. Turn **SELECT** within a zoomed musician to move through Part, Harmony, and Evolve.

| Page | Knob | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harmony | K1 Key | Transposes the band to a new tonic. During playback the change lands at the next chord. |
| Harmony | K2 Tempo | Adjusts the internal tempo. When following an external MIDI clock, the source controls the tempo. |
| Harmony | K3 Scale | Changes the scale used by harmony and the generator. During playback it lands at the next chord. |
| Harmony | K4 Meter | Chooses 3/4, 4/4 or 6/8. During playback it lands at the next bar. |
| Generator | K1 Genre | Chooses the band’s harmonic and rhythmic style. |
| Generator | K2 Tempo | Turn up for a faster beat. This is the same tempo control as on Harmony. |
| Generator | K3 Density | The former Energy control. At 50%, each musician uses its own Activity. At 60%, each free musician plays as if its Activity were 10 points higher, up to 100%. Held phrases keep their density. |
| Generator | K4 Evolve | Sets how often one musician automatically varies its phrase. 0% keeps the current phrases and cancels automatic edits waiting to land. It considers one musician every 8 bars at 1–49%, every 4 bars at 50–79%, and every 2 bars at 80–100%. Ambient doubles those intervals. Held or muted musicians and musicians with Activity below 35% are skipped. |

Open **Generator** with **SEL** and turn **SELECT** to **Groove**. **K1 Swing** delays alternating sixteenth notes in 3/4 and 4/4. At 0% the timing is straight, 67% gives a triplet feel, and 100% gives the strongest shuffle. It applies to generated drums, bass, layer phrases and rhythmic chord repeats. The ARP timing page controls arpeggiators. Swing is disabled in 6/8, where the beat already divides into three eighth notes.

**K2 Drums**, **K3 Bass**, and **K4 Chords** change the same Style settings you see on the musician panels. For example, in Lo-Fi you can choose **Half-time** drums, **Late pocket** bass, and **Held keys** from the Groove page.

Press **EDIT** on the Studio overview to shuffle the band’s progression, parts, key, scale, meter and tempo within the selected genre. The whole take lands at the next bar, or loads immediately with the band stopped. External MIDI clock supplies the tempo when selected. With a custom draft active, the band plays the draft’s harmony and meter at the current tempo.

Generated takes can move Bass down an octave and raise Chords or Layer. Chords or Layer may also pick up an arpeggio from the genre’s palette. The generator leaves space for the other musician and uses wider, slower patterns for Ambient Soundscape and tighter patterns for New Wave.

Zoom into a musician and press **EDIT** to regenerate that part on the next sixteenth-note grid point, or eighth-note grid point in 6/8. The rest of the band keeps playing the same song. Use **Sound → Tone** to choose a different instrument or drum kit.

### Custom progression

Press **SEL** on **Song** and turn **SELECT** to **Progression**. Press **EDIT** to open your draft. The first draft starts with a one-bar tonic chord. In the editor, **ALGORITHM** picks a chord slot and **SELECT** changes pages. The chord page edits degree, quality, color and length. The other pages expose timing, inversion, bass and phrase actions. On **Phrase**, choose Insert or Duplicate with **K1** and press **SEL** to add a slot.

Press **PLAY** in the editor to use the draft for all four musicians. If the band is already playing, the new progression starts at the next bar. The band keeps the draft’s chords, durations and meter when you regenerate a part. Drafts support 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8 in Studio and checkpoint using the existing draft storage when the unit is idle. To switch sources, open **Song → Progression**, turn **K1** to Bank or My draft, and press **SEL**.

### Musician panel

| Knob | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| K1 Style | Chooses between two ways of playing for the selected musician and genre. For example, Indie Bass offers Offbeat and Answer. |
| K2 Activity | Sets how busy that musician plays. The effect depends on the style and can include extra notes, accents and percussion hits. |
| K3 Level | Sets the musician’s mix level. This is the same level on Sound → Tone and Sound → Mix. |
| K4 Sustain on Chords | Sets the envelope level while a chord is held. Sound → Envelope → Release sets how long it rings after note-off. |
| K4 Touch on Bass and Layer | Sets the velocity of generated notes. 50% leaves the programmed velocity as played. |
| K4 Punch on Drums | Sets the velocity of generated drum hits. |

In **New Wave**, select **Bass → Moving pulse** to hear it follow Drums. A kick accents a bass note already on that step. An empty kick can pull a nearby bass note up to two steps earlier, leaving anchors and pickups in place. Switch Drums from **Machine** to **Open hats** to shift the kick and bass accent in the second bar. Evolve shows the resulting phrase under **Kick accents**. Bass follows the planned kick pattern through drum mutes and live fills. Hold keeps the current bass pattern when the drum groove changes.

In **Lo-Fi**, **Bass → Late pocket** places notes just behind kicks they share a step with. The delay follows the planned kick timing. The downbeat anchor, pickups, and offbeat notes keep their own timing. In 3/4 and 6/8, the groove carries the same kick and bass accents onto the shorter grid. Evolve labels the phrase **Behind the kick**. Hold keeps the current timing, and **Walk** plays its own phrase.

In **Indie**, select **Layer → Counterline** and **Chords → Chop** to hear the parts interact. Layer can move a note up to three grid steps to find room after a chord. It shortens the reply if the next chord is close. The Evolve page shows the resulting pattern under **Chord gaps**. Hold keeps that pattern while Chords changes. With **Ring**, Chords Band ARP, or a custom progression draft, Counterline plays its independent phrase.

Indie also has an eight-bar form. On bar 5, Layer rests and Drums leave out the hats while Chords, Bass, kick and snare carry the groove. The band comes back in on bar 6, then Drums play a turnaround in the second half of bar 8. The form starts over with each new take. It runs with Evolve at 0% and gives your live Fill, Drop and Return cues priority on Drums.

In **Lo-Fi**, **Layer → Fragments** plays a short reply in the first available space after the planned snare. It leaves the space open when a nearby key stab would crowd the reply. **Soft bell** keeps its own timing.

In **Dream Pop**, **Layer → Sparse motif** answers gaps in **Soft pulse** chords. With **Sustain**, the motif enters half a quarter-note after a harmonic change, on the next playable grid point. **Arp** keeps its own phrase.

In **Ambient Soundscape**, **Bass → Pedal** sustains the low note through the chord, and **Layer → Swells** brings in a soft chord tone after a harmonic change. The drums keep a quiet kick and leave space for a late percussion accent. **Slow contour** and **Distant notes** add more movement.

In **Pop Punk**, **Layer → Picked hook** plays short root and fifth notes in the gaps of the riff. **Octave answer** gives the second bar a higher reply. The last bar's tom turnaround follows the final shared bass-and-kick riff attack. Pickups and extra Double kick hits keep their own rhythm. Live drum cues take priority.

Style and Activity changes take effect on the next playable grid point. Continuous knobs take single steps when turned slowly and five-unit steps when turned quickly.

On the musician’s **Evolve** page, turn **K1 Hold** right to keep its current phrase through Density and automatic Evolve changes. Turn K1 left to return the musician to **Free** and apply the current Density setting. The two-bar Phrase display shows the generated pattern and its position in playback. The Studio panel shows **Held** while Hold is on. You can still edit Style or Activity or press **EDIT** to regenerate that musician. Hold then protects the edited phrase. Chord changes, arrangement cues, and live controls continue. Stop and PLAY keep Hold for the same take. A new band take or genre releases the holds when it lands.

### GLO Live

Press **GLO** to open the live mixer. **K1–K4** adjust Chords, Bass, Drums and Layer respectively. Each musician's mix level is shown as a percentage in both views. **ALGORITHM** switches between Map and State, and **SELECT** adjusts tempo. Press **GLO** or **HOME** to return to the previous panel, or **SAVE** for Settings.

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First four black keys | Toggle mute for Chords, Bass, Drums and Layer |
| First four white keys | Solo those musicians while held |
| D♯4 | Toggle percussion mute |
| Black keys 7–9 | Toggle kick, snare and hats |
| E4 / F4 | Unmute all / tap tempo |
| G4 / A4 | Fill while held / queue a one-bar fill |
| B4 | Drop or bring back drums at the next bar |
| C5 | Hold for a filtered noise riser, then release to fade it out |
| D5 | Hold to loop a slice of the mix. Turn **PRESETS** to change the repeat rate. |
| D♯5 | Hold for Hype distortion. |
| E5 | Hold for Ambience, a warm room with a stereo tail. Release to bring the mix forward while the room fades. |
| F5 | Hold for Radio, a thin, bright band-pass that sweeps across stereo. |
| F♯5 | Hold for a stereo Phaser. |
| G5 | Hold for Low Pass, a deep, bass-heavy sweep that moves across stereo. |

Tempo follows the external clock when MIDI clock input is active. From the zoomed Studio Drums panel, **PRESETS** left queues a fill and right drops or returns the drums at the next bar.

The riser gets louder and brighter over four quarter-note beats at the tempo when you press C5. Release it on a fill, drum drop, or section change.

Beat Repeat captures a mono slice of the mix and defaults to 1/16. While holding D5, turn **PRESETS** clockwise for shorter loops or counterclockwise for longer loops. The display shows the selected division. The range is 1/8 to 1/64, with the longest divisions available at higher tempos.

Radio and Low Pass build over about one beat and return to the mix as soon as you release the key. Ambience sends less of the Studio drums into the reverb so the notes can ring behind the beat.

The song continues underneath all seven effects, and the USB master mix captures them.

## Sound

Open **Mix** for Chords, Bass, Drums and Layer levels on K1–K4.

Open **Sound Library** at the end of the Sound rack. **K1 Library** selects the Genre list or All sounds. Highlight a musician and use **K1 Preset** to browse its tones or kits. With Genre selected, press **SEL** to load the featured palette. Press **EDIT** on the Sound overview or in Sound Library to shuffle tones, the drum kit, and FX presets. Each musician draws from its genre’s FX choices. Bass and Drums draw from shorter effects, leaving room for Chords or Layer to carry the longer delay or reverb. In a musician’s Tone panel, **EDIT** shuffles only that musician’s sound and FX, taking the other musicians’ effects and sends into account. While playing, the pitched sounds change immediately, FX crossfade, and the drum kit changes at the next bar. Sound EDIT keeps the song’s harmony and timing along with the current levels, pan and sends.

A highlighted Sound musician shows the Tone controls. Zoom in and turn **SELECT** to reach the other named pages. The page counter shows where you are.

| Page | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tone | Preset or drum kit | Mix level | First synth macro, such as filter cutoff on VA tones | Pan |
| Envelope | Attack, the time to reach full level | Decay, the time to fall toward Sustain | Sustain, the held level | Release, the time to fade after note-off |
| Sends | Drive amount | Chorus send | Delay send | Reverb send |
| FX Presets | Browse FX presets for this musician | | | |

Drums has Tone, Sends and FX Presets. **PRESETS** also browses the sound or kit. Sends shows a preview of the selected musician’s drive curve, chorus sweep, delay taps and reverb tail. Each graph responds to that musician’s knob. Press **FX** from any rack tab to open FX Presets. Turn **K1** or **PRESETS** to highlight a preset and press **SEL** to apply it to the highlighted musician. Turn **ALGORITHM** to switch musicians. Each musician keeps its own FX chain through patch changes, new takes, and restarts. Use **SELECT** to turn to Sends or another Sound page. **SAVE** opens preset management. Press **EDIT** on FX Presets to open the EQ and effects editor for the loaded preset. **LFO** from a zoomed pitched musician opens its detailed synth editor.

In Sound, highlight **Mix** to set part levels with **K1–K4**. Turn **ALGORITHM** one step past Mix to **FX Space** and use **K1** to scale the band’s chorus, delay and reverb together. Use the individual **Sends** pages to set which parts get each effect.

## Arpeggiator

Press **ARP** to open **Pattern**. Press **ARP** again to switch to **Timing**, and again to return to **Pattern**. Turn **SELECT** on either page to switch between Chords and Layer. The highlighted name on the faceplate shows whose arpeggiator you’re controlling. If Bass or Drums was selected, the panel opens on Chords. You can also find the ARP unit with **SEQ** and open it with **SEL**.

On **Pattern**, **K1 Trigger** sets the selected musician to Off, Keys, Band or Auto. Keys arpeggiates the notes you hold. Band plays that musician’s chord tones with your ARP settings. Auto shows the mode, octave span and rate chosen by the generator when the musician has an arpeggio. Selecting Off, Keys or Band overrides Auto for that musician. Chords and Layer share the manual pattern and timing settings. **K2 Mode** selects the note order or strum mode, **K3 Octave** sets the range, and **K4 Rate** sets the clock division or the spacing in milliseconds for a strum.

On **Timing**, **K1 Gate** sets how much of each step a note occupies, **K2 Swing** shifts alternating steps, **K3 Latch** keeps the arpeggio going after key release, and **K4 Glide** sets the Chords glide time.

## Record and Help

**Record** opens on **Saved Takes**. Press **REC** to record all four parts and press **REC** again to finish and save. Hold **REC** to discard the current recording. Turn **K1** to select a saved take and press **PLAY** to replay it. Press **PLAY** again to stop. Hold **SAVE** for three seconds to clear the selected take. Turn **ALGORITHM** to browse the four musician lanes and press **SEL** for a two-bar view. Use USB audio capture to record the full mix as audio.

**Help** contains Start here, Harmony, Playing and Capture. Turn **ALGORITHM** to select a topic and **SEL** to open it. The Harmony lesson shows the role of the note you play. **K1** switches between Auto and Manual harmony. **EDIT** opens Chord Lab.
