Make your first loop

  1. Generate a band. Press Start to enable audio. In Studio, turn PRESETS to pick a genre, press EDIT for a new arrangement, then press PLAY.
  2. Shape the groove. Press SEL on Generator. Turn SELECT to switch between Arrangement and Groove, where you can set swing and pick how the musicians play.
  3. Play it live. Press GLO while the band plays. Use the keys to solo parts, cue fills or drops, and hold an upper key to play an effect.

Explore the Studio

Studio rack

Turn ALGORITHM to move between Generator, Song, Harmony, Progression, ARP and the four musicians.

Sounds and sends

Open Sound to select tones and kits, shape envelopes, and set drive, chorus, delay and reverb sends.

Your progression

Open the Progression unit and press EDIT to write a draft. Press PLAY inside the editor to use it with the band.
