RAKT / Field guide

Your first jam

Make a band, shape what it plays, then take it live. Keep these illustrated guides open as you play.

  1. Generate

    In Studio, pick a genre with PRESETS. Press EDIT for a new arrangement, then PLAY to hear the band.

  2. Shape

    Press SEL on Generator. Turn SELECT to Groove for swing and the musicians’ playing styles.

  3. Perform

    Press GLO while the band plays. Solo a part, cue a fill or drop, or hold an upper key to play an effect.
