Make your first loop

  1. Start the band. Press Start to enable audio, then press PLAY. Try a few keys over the groove.
  2. Browse the rack. Turn ALGORITHM to choose a musician or outboard unit. Press SEL to zoom in and use the controls shown on screen.
  3. Make it yours. Turn PRESETS to change the genre. Turn SELECT on the overview to reach Sound, then open Mix for the four channel levels.

Explore the Studio

Outboard

Press SEQ to reach Harmony, Shaping, Genre, ARP and Progression.

Sounds and sends

Open Sound to select tones and kits, shape envelopes, and set drive, chorus, delay and reverb sends.

Your progression

Open the Progression unit and press EDIT to write a draft. Press PLAY inside the editor to use it with the band.
